About Meshwark

Meshwark is a digital car rental marketplace that connects renters and agencies across Egypt — especially in governorates often overlooked by traditional rental platforms.

What We Do

We offer an easy-to-use platform where users can browse, compare, and book vehicles with or without drivers. Our platform empowers individuals and rental companies to list their vehicles and earn income, while giving users in smaller cities better access to flexible, affordable transportation.

Our Mission

To make car rental accessible, trustworthy, and convenient for everyone — no matter where they are in Egypt.

Our Vision

To be the leading car rental platform in underserved regions, bringing transportation freedom to millions and turning idle cars into profitable assets.
